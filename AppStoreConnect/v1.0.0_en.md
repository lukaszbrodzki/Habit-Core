## Description

Small steps, big changes. Build habits that actually stick.

Habit Core is a simple, clear habit tracker. Every day is another tile on your progress grid — the more color, the better you're doing. No clutter and no pressure: just you, your goals and progress you can see.

Key Features:
- Focus: everything you need to do today in one place. Check habits off with a single tap, with the most urgent ones always on top.
- Flexible schedules: daily, weekly, monthly or every N days — with optional start and end dates.
- Progress grids: every habit gets its own grid with completions, success rate and longest streak.
- All habits in one grid: see how you're doing overall, how many perfect days you've had and how long your streak is.
- Count only recent: focus on your current form — stats can cover just your most recent occurrences, so an old slip doesn't drag your score down.
- Home Screen widget: the history of one habit or all of them, always at hand.
- Smart reminder: one daily notification at the time you choose — only when something is still left to do.
- iCloud sync: your habits on all your devices.
- Archive: put a habit aside and bring it back later with its full history.
- Your style: habit colors, your own order, and a light, dark or system theme.

Privacy first: no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your private iCloud.
